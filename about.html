About

For this project I was really inspired by the lore behind fairy rings/fairy circles. The idea that if you stepped within a fairy ring, you were supposively transported into the "fairy world" or some other dimension. I decided to have a more childlike innocence approach to the theme of the story, where the main character meets a helpful friend that helps her get back into reality. I was inspired loosely around the story of Alice in Wonderland, as well as general fantasy stories and characters. Thank you so much for reading my story!
